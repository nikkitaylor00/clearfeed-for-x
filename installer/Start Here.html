Set up Clearfeed for X

Hide promoted posts in your Chrome feed.

Add Clearfeed to Chrome

  1. Open Google Chrome and enter chrome://extensions in its address bar. The macOS installer opens that page in a separate tab.
  2. Turn on Developer mode and click Load unpacked.
  3. Copy the folder path below. In Chrome’s folder picker, press ⌘⇧G, paste the path, press Return, and click Select.

Finish on X

Chrome opens Clearfeed’s welcome page after you load it. Follow the steps there to pin the toolbar button and check your connection on X.

Chrome loads the extension from the folder you select. Keep that folder in place.
